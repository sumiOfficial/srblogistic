Core Specialization

Reefer (Refrigerated) Fleet Transportation

SRB Logistic operates a dedicated fleet of temperature-controlled refrigerated trucks ranging from 14ft urban distribution units to 32ft multi-axle super reefers. Every container is constructed with 100mm high-density polyurethane foam (PUF) insulation and powered by world-class Carrier Transicold and Thermo King refrigeration units.

Continuous Thermal Logging: Calibrated electronic sensors stream temperature readouts every 180 seconds to the centralized control tower.
Dual-Evaporator Partitioning: Ability to transport two distinct temperature zones (e.g., deep freeze -18°C and chilled +4°C) simultaneously in a single truck.
Turnaround Punctuality: Pre-cooling protocols completed prior to docking to eliminate loading thermal shock.

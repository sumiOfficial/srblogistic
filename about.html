Company Profile

Architecting India's Most Reliable
Cold Chain Logistic Highway

Rooted in Narnaul, Haryana, SRB Logistic was founded to solve India’s fundamental cold chain bottlenecks. We combine advanced reefer technology, zero-breakdown fleet engineering, and 24/7 IoT telemetry to ensure that perishable food and life-saving pharmaceuticals reach every corner of India in pristine condition.

Our Strategic Genesis

The Narnaul Advantage: Gateway to Delhi-Mumbai Arteries

Situated at Yaduvanshi Mode, Rewari Road, Narnaul, SRB Logistic commands a prime geographic position in Southern Haryana. Bordering Rajasthan and directly feeding into the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC), Kundli-Manesar-Palwal (KMP) Expressway, and Trans-Haryana Highway (NH-152D), our fleet operates at the geographical heart of North India's agro-industrial manufacturing engine.

By eliminating traditional third-party broker friction and investing in dedicated multi-axle reefer containers equipped with real-time digital sensors, we have delivered over 50,000 flawless shipments for dairy giants, ice cream distributors, quick-service restaurant (QSR) chains, and healthcare leaders.

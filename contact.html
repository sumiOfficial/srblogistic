Frequently Asked Questions

Common answers regarding temperature compliance, transit SLAs, and booking.

Can clients monitor the container temperature in real time?

Yes. Every SRB Logistic consignment is paired with a live tracking link. Clients can view live chamber temperature, truck GPS location, speed, and battery status directly from any browser or mobile device.

What happens in the rare event of a mechanical breakdown on the expressway?

Our 32ft and 24ft reefers are engineered with high-density 100mm PUF thermal insulation capable of maintaining set temperatures for up to 12 hours without active power. Additionally, our 24/7 Control Tower dispatches our nearest mobile maintenance unit or transfers cargo to a standby rescue vehicle stationed along major arterial corridors.

How quickly can SRB Logistic place a truck for loading?

For standard lanes originating from Haryana, Delhi-NCR, Rajasthan, and Western India, we offer placement within 2 to 4 hours of confirmed booking. For Pan-India dedicated contracts, vehicles are permanently pre-staged at client manufacturing facilities.
